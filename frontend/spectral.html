How to read this

Aperture photometry with a local background; heavily flagged points are rejected. No aperture correction, so fluxes are lower limits.

Flag 0 or 2097152 = nominal. ≥ 4294967296 = bad pixel involved. Be cautious near the D3/D4 dichroic.
